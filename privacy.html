Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: what you type into When can I stop? stays on your device. There is no account, no cookie, no analytics and no tracking, and the page loads nothing from other sites. The only data that reaches anyone is what every browser sends to fetch a web page, and it never contains your plans.

Who runs this site

whencanistop.fyi is run by Gabriele Ardizzone, in Switzerland, as a free, non-commercial project. For anything about privacy, write to privacy@whencanistop.fyi.

What stays on your device

The simulation runs in your browser. The page keeps these in the browser's local storage, on your device only, and never sends them anywhere:

  • your saved plans, and the plan on screen with its unsaved changes;
  • your settings: the random seed, the number of simulated futures, the number format, which sections of the form are open, and whether you closed the welcome note;
  • when you last exported your plans, for the reminder to keep a backup.

They're there for what you ask the page to do: keep your plans, and carry on where you left off. They stay until you delete them, in the page or by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

Once the site has loaded, a service worker also keeps a copy of the site's own files, so that it works offline. It holds nothing about you. The site sets no cookies.

Files and share links

Export plans saves a file on your device. A share link carries one plan inside the link itself, after the "#", which browsers never send to a server: the link goes only where you send it. Anyone who has it can open the plan, unless you lock it with a password.

What the host receives

The site is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Like any web host, Cloudflare receives what your browser sends to fetch each file: your IP address, which file was asked for, when, and browser details (the user agent).

  • Cloudflare uses this to deliver the site and to protect it from attacks, and keeps it in its logs, as its privacy policy describes.
  • This site also has Cloudflare's Workers Logs turned on, to find problems. Whatever they record about a request is kept for 3 days, can be read only in the site's Cloudflare account, and isn't used for anything else.

Cloudflare does this on the operator's behalf, under its data processing addendum. It has data centres around the world and is based in the USA. Transfers rely on Cloudflare's certification under the EU-U.S. and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and on the EU standard contractual clauses in its data processing addendum, which also cover data protected by Swiss law.

For visitors protected by the GDPR, the legal basis is the operator's legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to other sites

The page links to GitHub, for the code, and to Buy Me a Coffee. Nothing is loaded from them unless you click a link; once you're there, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you the operator has, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use: write to privacy@whencanistop.fyi. In practice the operator has nothing about you beyond those few days of request logs.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU or the EEA, the authority where you live.

No advice, no warranty

When can I stop? is a tool for thinking about a plan. It is not financial advice and not a forecast. It is free, and provided as it is, without any warranty (MIT license). What you decide about your money is up to you.
